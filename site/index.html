What it does

🖼️ Reads text in images

A poster with "message me privately" has no typed letters. The bot reads it with OCR, without a GPU, in 0.6 s.

💸 Understands the scam

The "I gave 500 and got 5,000 back" testimonial, the fake happy customer, the crypto giveaway that sends you elsewhere.

🎭 Sees through the disguise

Joining with a normal name and switching it before posting: it watches newcomers for 7 days.

🔐 Spots stolen accounts

A member who went quiet for months and comes back with a spam bio is banned for safety, and the group is told why.

🧠 Learns on its own

From your decisions, from safe bans and from the links of banned spammers. No external AI: it all stays on your server.

🔗 Banned in one, banned in all

One ban covers all your groups, and it checks the worldwide CAS and lols.bot spammer lists.

🤝 Respects normal people

Trust levels from 1 to 10, doubtful cases in private with buttons, and a test mode that bans no one.

🌍 For any community

English and Spanish built in, any other language is one JSON file. You choose the alphabets your group uses.

Install it on your server

You need a Linux machine that stays on (a cheap VPS, a mini PC or a home server). It uses less than 100 MB of RAM.

  1. Create your bot with @BotFather and get your Telegram user id.
  2. Install Docker, download the code and fill in .env.
  3. Start it with docker compose up -d and add it as admin to your groups.

It also runs on a Raspberry Pi with a 64-bit system, using the ready-made image.

FAQ

Can I just add @CazaSpamBot to my group?

No: that copy is private and only moderates its owner's groups. The code is free, so you run your own bot with your own token, and it's fully yours. It takes about 5 minutes.

Will it ban legitimate members?

That's what it tries hardest to avoid. Members with history in the group are protected by their trust level, doubtful cases reach you with buttons instead of ending in a ban, and you can start in test mode.

How is it different from a captcha bot?

A captcha only stops dumb bots at the door. Today's spam gets past it and then posts at leisure. CazaSpamBot looks at who they are and what they post, days later too.

Does my data leave my server?

No. Learning and OCR run locally. Outside Telegram, the only things that go out are lookups to the CAS and lols.bot spammer lists, a daily check on GitHub for new versions (anonymous, and you can turn it off) and, if you turn them on, reports to Telegram.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. The interface ships in English and Spanish, and adding a language is one JSON file.
